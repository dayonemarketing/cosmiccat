How a visit works

Three steps, about ninety seconds to explain

1

Grab tokens

Buy at the counter or online before you come. Tokens never expire, so whatever your kid does not spend today is waiting for them next time.

Is it safe for little kids?

Yes. There is one way in and out, our team members are on the floor the entire time, and the room is small enough that you can see your child from anywhere in it. Machines are anchored and everything in here is built for small hands.

Are the machines rigged?

Our claws hold the same strength on every play, the first of the day and the last. Difficulty comes from where the plush is sitting and how well you line the claw up. Here is the full explanation.

What will this actually cost me?

Most families spend $20, which is 25 tokens and roughly an hour for two kids. Come in with $5 and have a great time, or settle in for the whole afternoon. It is entirely up to you.

When is it quietest?

It stays pretty calm in here. Weekday mornings right after we open at 11 are the quietest hours of all, which families with younger or more sensitive kids tend to prefer.

Do I have to play too?

Only if you want to. There is seating, and plenty of parents settle in with a coffee while the kids work the wall. If you do want a turn, the machines are just as fun at 40 as they are at 8.

Can we bring a stroller?

Yes. Aisles fit a standard double stroller. Restrooms are on site and parking is free right outside Suite 180.
