The Squishy Bar, explained

A squishy is a soft, squeezable toy about the size of your palm. Kids collect them, line them up, name them and carry them everywhere. The Squishy Bar is our craft table where your child builds a whole set of their own, and it is the most popular hour of our week.

1

Decorate the box

Every child starts with a plain keepsake box and a table covered in colorful gems and charms. They design it however they like, and a host is right there for the fiddly parts.

2

Shop the bar

Then they walk the Squishy Bar and choose their own squishies to fill it. Five for a Full Box, three for a Mini. Your child picks every single one.

3

Take on the arcade

Box in hand, they have the run of the claw machine floor for the rest of the visit. Most kids go straight for the machines to add to the haul.

What ages is this good for?

Most kids at the table are between 3 and 10. Younger ones do beautifully with a grown-up sitting alongside, and older kids tend to take the decorating very seriously.

Do I need to stay with my child?

A host runs the table the whole time, so you are welcome to sit nearby with a coffee. Plenty of parents end up joining in anyway.

Can they pick any squishies they want?

Yes. The whole bar is open to them, and they choose their own five or three. Half the fun is watching them deliberate.

Can a group book together?

Yes. Seats are limited per session, so book the group at once and we will keep you together at the same table.

Party questions

The things people email about

Can we bring our own cake?

Yes. Cake, cupcakes, pizza, whatever you want. We provide the table, plates and the cleanup.

What if fewer kids show up than I booked?

The tokens are yours either way. We load them onto the birthday parent's account, so they are waiting for you on your next visit.

Is the deposit refundable?

Fully refundable up to 7 days out. Inside 7 days we will happily move it to another date for you.

Do adults count toward the guest number?

No. Bring as many parents as you like. Guest counts are kids playing.

Can we book on a Saturday?

Party tables, yes, any day we are open. Private buyouts run weekdays, before we open and after we close, so you get the whole room to yourselves.
