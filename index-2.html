What happens

An hour, start to finish

  1. Pick your box

    Every child gets their own keepsake box to make theirs. Mini or full size, the craft is the same.

  2. Bedazzle it

    Gems, charms and stickers cover the table. There is no right way to do it and no limit on how much sparkle goes on.

  3. Choose your squishies

    Three or five, chosen by the child, packed into the box they just made, and carried out the door.

Good to know

Before you book

Weekend mornings only

Saturdays and Sundays, with two sessions: 9:00 to 10:00 AM and 10:00 to 11:00 AM. The arcade itself opens at 11:00 AM, so you can make a morning of it.

Walk in later if you miss the morning

The tables stay set up all day. Come by any time after 12:30 PM and join in without a reservation.

Everything is included

Box, gems, charms and squishies are all supplied. Bring your child and nothing else.

25 seats a session

Both box options share the same 25 seats, so once a session fills it closes for both. Booking ahead is the safe move for a weekend morning.
