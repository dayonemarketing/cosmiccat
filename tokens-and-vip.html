Token questions

Before you buy

Do tokens expire?

Never. Whatever is on your account is still waiting whenever you come back, months or years from now.

Can I use tokens I bought online right away?

Yes. Buy them from the car in the parking lot if you want. They load to your account and the counter scans your phone.

How many plays is a token?

One play on most machines, two on a small number of premium machines. Those are marked right on the glass so you always know before you play.

Can two kids share one account?

Yes, and most families do exactly that. One parent account, everybody plays off it.

Do you take cash?

Yes, at the counter. Online is card only.
