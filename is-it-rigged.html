Are your claw machines rigged?

Short answer: no. Here is exactly how ours are set, in plain language, so you know what you are paying for before you put a token in.

How claw machines work

Every claw machine has a grip setting. Here is where ours sits.

Claw machines ship with an adjustable grip. Whoever runs the machine chooses how firmly the claw closes, and that single setting shapes how the whole room feels to play. It is worth understanding, because it is the honest answer to the question in the headline.

Ours are set to grip at full strength on every play, and we leave them there. Line the claw up well and it holds. That is the whole trick, and it is the same trick for everybody who walks in.

How ours are set

  • ✓Every play gets the same claw strength, the first of the day and the last.
  • ✓Difficulty comes from where the plush is sitting and how you line up the claw.
  • ✓A machine plays exactly the same right after someone wins as it did before.
  • ✓If a machine is not playing right, tell a Cosmic Cat team member and the token is on us.

Machine not playing right?

Tell a Cosmic Cat team member and we will replace the token on the spot. Just ask. A team member will assess the machine and get it back to perfect working order.

Actually useful

Four things that genuinely improve your odds

Play the edge, not the middle

A plush half hanging off the pile only needs a nudge to tip your way. The one buried in the middle takes a lot more work.

Aim for the tag, not the belly

Claws hold a seam or a tag far better than they hold a smooth round body.

Watch one play first

Ten seconds of watching someone else tells you a lot about how that particular pile is sitting.

Ask us

Our team members know which machines are playing loose today and they will happily point you at one. We would much rather you win.
